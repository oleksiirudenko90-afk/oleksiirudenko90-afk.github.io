zeromail

A personal Gmail app for the Mac (keyboard-first) and the iPhone.

zeromail is a private app made by one person for their own use. It isn't sold, offered to the public or available to download.

It connects to Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Contacts (read-only) with Google sign-in so its owner can read, sort and answer their own mail on their Mac and iPhone. Mail is only ever sent between those devices and Google. There is no zeromail server.
