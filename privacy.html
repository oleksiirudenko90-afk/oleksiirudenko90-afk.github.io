Privacy policy

zeromail · Effective 8 October 2026

zeromail is a personal Gmail app for macOS, made by one person for their own use. It isn't sold or offered to the public. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What zeromail accesses

After you sign in with Google and give permission, zeromail accesses:

  • Gmail: your messages, labels and drafts, so it can show, sort, archive, label and send mail when you ask it to.
  • Google Calendar: your events, so it can show your day next to an invitation, answer invitations and add bookings to your calendar when you ask it to.
  • Basic profile: your name, email address and profile photo, to show which account you're using.

How the data is used

  • Only to provide zeromail's features to you on your own Mac.
  • zeromail never sends, deletes, changes or answers anything unless you ask it to.
  • No advertising, no profiling, no analytics and no tracking.

Where the data is stored

  • A copy of your mail and settings is stored only on your Mac, in zeromail's app folder, so it opens instantly and works offline.
  • Sign-in tokens are encrypted with a key kept in the macOS Keychain.
  • Data travels only between your Mac and Google, over encrypted connections (HTTPS). There is no zeromail server, and no data is sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • The optional "Write with AI" feature runs entirely on your Mac using Apple's built-in on-device model. No email text leaves your Mac for it.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone, and is not used to train AI or machine learning models.

Google API Services

zeromail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • In zeromail (Settings → Accounts), Sign out revokes the app's access to that account, and Remove deletes the account's data stored on your Mac.
  • You can also remove zeromail's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the app and its app folder removes everything it stored.
